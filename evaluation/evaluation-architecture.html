🎯
Faithfulness (0–1)
Measures whether every claim in the answer is supported by the retrieved context. The primary anti-hallucination metric. Uses LLM to decompose the answer into statements and verify each against context.
Target: ≥ 0.85 | Critical for compliance use case
💬
Answer Relevancy (0–1)
Measures how relevant the answer is to the question. Uses reverse question generation — creates questions from the answer, then measures cosine similarity to the original question.
Target: ≥ 0.80 | Low score = answer drifted off-topic
🔍
Context Precision (0–1)
Of the retrieved context chunks, what fraction are actually relevant to the question? High precision = retrieval is clean and not noisy. Measures retrieval quality.
Target: ≥ 0.75 | Low = too much irrelevant context retrieved
📚
Context Recall (0–1)
Does the retrieved context contain all information needed to answer correctly (as per ground truth)? Verifies that the retrieval step finds complete information.
Target: ≥ 0.80 | Low = retrieval missing important chunks
✅
Answer Correctness (0–1)
Semantic similarity between the generated answer and the ground truth answer. Combines factual overlap and semantic embedding similarity. The end-to-end quality metric.
Target: ≥ 0.70 | End-to-end correctness benchmark
🔑
Keyword Coverage (Manual, 0–1)
Fraction of expected domain keywords (e.g. "STR", "FIU-IND", "25%") that appear in the answer. A fast, offline check that doesn't require an LLM to compute.
Target: ≥ 0.70 | Good proxy for factual completeness
