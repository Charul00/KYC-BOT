SECTION 3: COMPANY OVERVIEW & BUSINESS DESCRIPTION
================================================================================

eClerx Services Limited is a global leader in productized services, transforming businesses with advanced technology, artificial intelligence, and deep domain expertise to deliver exceptional outcomes. Founded in 2000, the company has grown from a niche business process outsourcing firm into a comprehensive digital services powerhouse serving Fortune 500 companies and leading global enterprises.

The company specializes in providing knowledge process outsourcing (KPO) and business process management (BPM) services. eClerx combines deep domain expertise with advanced technology capabilities including artificial intelligence, machine learning, robotic process automation, and data analytics to deliver high-value services that drive measurable business outcomes for its clients.

eClerx's approach is centered on productized service delivery, which means standardizing complex processes into repeatable, scalable solutions that can be deployed across multiple clients and industries. This approach enables faster implementation, consistent quality, and continuous improvement through data-driven insights.
